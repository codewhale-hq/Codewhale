Codewhale Dot Glyphs

The 48 x 48 bitmap masks in pet/src/core/dot-glyphs.ts are rasterized from
Noto Sans CJK SC Medium, Version 2.004. This bitmap glyph asset is provided

The full source font is not bundled. Reproduce with:
python3 pet/scripts/build-dot-glyphs.py /path/to/NotoSansCJKsc-Medium.otf
Generator dependency: Pillow. Recorded renderer: Pillow 11.3.0,
FreeType 2.13.3.
The generator verifies the source font SHA-256 before rasterization.

Each mask is 48 rows of 48 ASCII characters. '#' means ink; '.' means empty.
Coordinates are row-major, with x increasing right and y increasing down.
Phrases use the largest fitting font size, are centered without stretching,
have at least two empty pixels of padding, and use a coverage threshold of 128.

The generator reads the English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and
Japanese status and tool labels from crates/localization/locales/. The same
typed activity mapping is used by the terminal caption. Chinese labels of up
to three characters occupy one line; longer phrases occupy two centered
lines. English wraps at spaces and Japanese at phrase boundaries. No phrase
is abbreviated to a single character. Captions retain the full meaning in
the interface language independently of particle density, motion and color.
完成 is only for observed successful completion.
