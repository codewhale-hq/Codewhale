# Site colors and the Moda brand kit

Decision (founder, 2026-10-11): the Moda brand kit "Codewhale"
(`bk_3S5VAJCR69901RXWE5S5DFR3B3`) is the source of truth for color. The site's
role tokens in `app/styles/tokens-roles.css` paint with `--kit-*` (the kit's
original 25 colors) and nothing else. Geometry (radii, focus, spacing) still
comes from the generated `--gpui-*` tokens.

## What was not changed, and why

`app/tokens.css` is generated by `scripts/export-design-tokens.py` from
`crates/palette/src/rgb.rs` (the terminal palettes) and
`vendor/codewhale-design/tokens.json`. Its 131 off-kit colors (`--whale-*`,
`--light-*`, `--shoreline-*`) are the terminal's palettes. No page, component
or stylesheet reads them (grep of `app`, `components`, `lib`); they only feed
the palette contract tests. They are not site colors and are left to the
palette crate. The `--gpui-*` colors in that file are no longer read by any
role; they remain generated for parity checks.

The `#0a1e33` / `#f6f2e8` pair in `primitives.css` is the captured terminal
frame's real colors and stays as captured.

## Contrast (WCAG 2.x, computed from the kit hexes)

Every text ink on every ground (bg, surface, panel, hover, selected):

| Scheme | Lowest text ratio | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| light | 5.18 | Signal Blue on Shallows |
| dark and stage | 4.68 | Coral on Night Border |

Other pairs: light line-strong 6.81 on Warm Paper, 7.18 on White; dark
line-strong 9.13 on Deep Navy, 7.00 on Ocean Depth; white on the primary fill
6.18 (Signal Blue) and 7.90 (Whale Blue); on-accent 6.18 light, 8.76 dark;
Foam on the sea ramp 6.75 / 12.89 / 13.15; Mist on the sea ramp 4.68 / 8.94 /
9.13 (the lowest is Mist on Whale Blue). `lib/gpui-role-tokens.test.ts` asserts
these floors in CI.

Non-text edges: the kit's hairlines are faint by design (Tideline on Warm
Paper 1.31:1, Night Border on Deep Navy 1.65:1). Control edges use
`--line-strong`, never `--line`.

## Gaps in the kit (recorded, not edited)

1. A control-edge color for each scheme. Slate and Mist pass 3:1 but are
   heavier than a border should be. Proposal: light `#7A8B95` (3.35:1 on Warm
   Paper, 3.53:1 on White, 3.10:1 on Seaglass); dark `#6F8CA3` (4.37:1 on Deep
   Navy, 3.35:1 on Ocean Depth, 3.47:1 on Night Surface).
2. The kit has no ordered ramp of five dark grounds; Night Border doubles as
   the selected-row fill and the hairline.
3. Fonts: the kit names Noto Sans; the site loads Shannon Sans subsets. Not a
   color, not changed here.

## Where codewhale-design differs

`vendor/codewhale-design/tokens.json` (GPUI 1.1.2) uses neutral warm grays
(#28292b text, #faf8f5 paper, #202123 dark background) and the kit uses
blue-tinted inks and navy grounds. The kit now leads on the site; the GPUI
tokens differ on text, muted, lines, sidebar, dark grounds, live, attention and
danger by a few units of lightness. Primary light (#245bc7) and danger light
(#9e3f36) are identical. That repo was not edited.
